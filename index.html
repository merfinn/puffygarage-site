Build it. Paint it. Drive it!

Kids build and paint their own cars, pick a fluffy driver and hit the road, alone or with a grown-up on one screen.

See it on the App Store

What's inside

10 cars to build

Police car, fire truck, digger, monster truck, race car and more. Change wheels, roof, front, back and stickers.

Paint every part

Pick a colour for each part, or draw with crayons and use the drawing as paint.

Ten puff drivers

Teddy, Bunbun, Mochi, Pandy, Ribbit and friends, with hats and glasses.

Four roads to drive

Drag a finger to stay on the road, collect stars and look out for the truck.

Two players, one screen

A child and a grown-up drive side by side with two fingers.

My Car's Day widget

Your own car gets washed, goes exploring and sleeps through the day.

For grown-ups

No ads, no accounts and no data collection. It works without internet. Sharing and purchases sit behind a grown-ups gate. The police car and fire truck are free, and one in-app purchase unlocks the other 8 cars and two-player races. There is no subscription.
